Known limitations
  • A phone or laptop camera stands in for the drone camera. The same software would run on a real drone's video.
  • Bad or coloured lighting changes the colours and can give a wrong score.
  • It needs a plain, light background. Soil or a messy background confuses it.
  • It detects that a leaf looks unhealthy, but cannot always tell the cause.
  • The AI model was trained on a small number of our own leaf photos, so it can make mistakes on other plants.
